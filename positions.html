Positions

Note. Currently, there are no vacancies for PhD, post-doc, or Research Fellow positions. Only self-funded Master's and PhD students are considered.

PhD applications

Open for new recruitment. To apply, please send an email (150 words maximum) titled “PhD application (auxeticslab)” and include:

  • your CV,
  • transcripts (Bachelor's and Master's),
  • the topic (metamaterials or auxetics),
  • your expertise in computational/continuum mechanics or machine learning, and
  • your one best publication.

Mandatory requirements

The minimum eligibility requirements are:

  • Must be from a non-sanctioned country (e.g., applicants from Iran are not accepted),
  • first-class honours or equivalent, and
  • three peer-reviewed journal publications (preferably as the first author).

Desired skills

We actively look for talented PhD students; a suitable topic can be defined according to any of the following skills:

  • Classic/generalised continuum mechanics
  • Computational mechanics

Topics for collaborative research

Multi-layered semi-auxetics for energy-absorbing applications

Micromechanical models are considered in conjunction with computational techniques and experimental validation. Developing new continuum mechanical models is also of interest.
